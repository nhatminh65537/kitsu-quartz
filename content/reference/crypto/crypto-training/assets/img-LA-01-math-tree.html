Mathematics of Cryptography — Reference Map
🔢 Lý thuyết số
  • Số nguyên tố, GCD, EEA
  • φ(n) — Euler phi, Fermat/Euler thm
  • Chinese Remainder Theorem (CRT)
  • Quadratic residues, Legendre
  • p-adic numbers (Hensel lifting)
⚡ Hard: Factoring, DLP
⚙️ Đại số trừu tượng
  • Nhóm (Group): cyclic, abelian, order
  • Vành (Ring): Z/nZ, polynomial rings
  • Trường (Field): GF(p), GF(2ⁿ)
  • Extension fields, characteristic
  • Galois theory, automorphisms
⚡ Hard: DLP in groups
〰️ Đường cong Elliptic
  • Weierstrass eq., group law
  • Hasse theorem, torsion points
  • Endomorphism ring, Frobenius
  • Weil / Tate / Ate Pairing
  • Isogeny, Deuring correspondence
⚡ Hard: ECDLP (no Index Calculus)
🔷 Đại số tuyến tính & Lattice
  • Vector spaces over 𝔽p, basis
  • Lattice trong Rⁿ, determinant
  • Gram-Schmidt orthogonalization
  • SVP / CVP problems
  • LLL / BKZ reduction algorithms
⚡ Hard: SVP, LWE, SIS (PQC!)
🎲 Xác suất & Thông tin
  • Shannon entropy H(X)
  • Perfect secrecy, mutual info
  • Statistical / Total variation dist.
  • Negligible / overwhelming prob.
  • Birthday paradox → collision
📐 Foundation: Security proofs
⏱️ Độ phức tạp tính toán
  • P, NP, BPP — classical classes
  • BQP — quantum complexity class
  • One-Way Functions (OWF)
  • Average-case vs. worst-case
  • Reduction, PPT adversary model
⚡ Hard: Factoring, DLP, LWE all in NP∩coNP
↓ ↓ ↓
RSA ← Number Theory

ECC/ECDSA ← Elliptic Curves
Kyber/Dilithium ← Lattice
ZK-SNARKs ← EC + Pairing
