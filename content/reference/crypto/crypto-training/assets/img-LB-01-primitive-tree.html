🎲 CSPRNG / DRBG
CTR_DRBG (NIST) ★SP 800-90A. AES-CTR core. HSM, TLS nonce, FIPS-certified platforms
HMAC_DRBGRFC 6979 deterministic ECDSA nonce. SHA-2 core. Widely deployed
ChaCha20-basedLinux /dev/urandom, arc4random() — fast, no AES-NI dependency
Dual_EC_DRBG BACKDOORNSA backdoor 2006-2014. NIST withdrawn 2014. Never use.
