Why parallel repetition fails
Identification protocols with small challenge space use ω repetitions to reduce soundness error exponentially. But in the blind signature setting, each repetition gives the adversary an extra binary degree of freedom (choose aux⁰ or aux¹). With ℓ = ωξ sessions (ξ = ⌈log|C|⌉), adversary gains enough bits to decompose the target hash — breaking OMUF despite repetition.
