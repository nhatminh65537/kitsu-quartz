→ Schnorr blind sig broken in practice
OMUF break Solving ROS gives ℓ+1 valid Schnorr blind signatures after only ℓ signing interactions. Adversary opens ℓ sessions, uses the ℓ hash values c*_1,…,c*_ℓ to construct the target row, and outputs one extra forgery without any additional session.
Key insight The overdetermined system (ℓ+1 equations, ℓ unknowns) has a solution because the adversary freely chooses the coefficient vectors ρ̂_i — she is not forced to use specific commitments. This freedom is exactly what concurrent sessions provide and sequential sessions deny.
